About KRITZAAR

KRITZAAR is a free, dark-mode content hub. Read stories, play games, follow AI topics, and find cool things, all in one place. There is no sign-up and nothing to install, and every article or game opens right inside the site.

What you will find here

StoriesMystery stories and real-life cases, from unsolved disappearances to ghost tales.GamesFree HTML games you can play instantly in your browser, with no download or sign-up.Cool StuffFree tools and mini projects, such as an image compressor, plus small games and experiments.AIArticles about artificial intelligence: what is new, how it works and how people use it.InterestingDiscoveries and curiosities that are worth knowing about.

How to use it

  • Browse: pick a section from the bar at the top of the site, or let the preview strip show you around.
  • Search: type in the search box to find articles and games across every section. Searching a topic such as games also shows that section and what it is about.
  • Save for later: tap Save on an article to keep it in your Saved tab.
  • Share: use Share on any article or section to send a link to a friend.
  • Play: tap Play on a game card, and use Fullscreen for the best view. If a game does not load, use Retry or open it in a new tab.

Privacy and ads

You do not need an account. Your Saved items and some cached content are stored only in your own browser, on your device. KRITZAAR shows ads through Google AdSense, which may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings.

About political content

The Political section is for information only. KRITZAAR does not promote or endorse any political party, candidate or ideology, and shows a notice before you enter it.
